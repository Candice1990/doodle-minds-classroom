TEACHER CONTROLS · AGES 5–7

One idea: an instruction tells Pip what to do.

Standalone lesson, ages 5–7. The computer cannot guess a missing instruction. Use the recurring question “Which instruction is missing?” Invite children to point, name the action and explain what changed after adding it. Read each mission aloud. Run the initial short program before choosing a repair. An empty space does nothing. Compare the actual result with the goal, add one picture and run from a fresh starting state.

Five cases: hat missing Put on; dirty apple missing Wash; cupcake missing Icing; dark lantern missing Light on; escaping balloon missing Tie. Start with teacher-guided observation and fade prompts. The two remaining cards are supplied, so children can focus on the missing action. Do not require English reading. Point to the pictures and act out the commands.

Allow about 5 minutes greeting/overview, 28 minutes exploring, 20 minutes partner making/testing and 7 minutes sharing. Use the paper kit: test a complete three-card plan, remove a card, have a partner execute the short plan, then repair and retest. Use pretend food/tools. Decorate after testing. Support with two candidate cards; extend by moving the repaired card and explaining why position matters.

Stop cancels playback safely. Try another fix restores that task’s short program. Slower pace gives more observation time. Sound uses the original recorded Hello and approved natural feedback. Open the printable repair kit ↗.
