Partner test

A · Program maker

Show your plan and program. Predict. Watch without giving extra instructions.

B · Program tester

Follow every picture, one at a time. Record what happened. Compare with the plan.

Together

Find a difference. Make a little change. Test all four steps again. Swap roles.
